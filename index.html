How I work

I design for the person on the other side.

For a player mid-fight, that means a UI they can read in half a second. For someone using a tool at work, it means never having to think about the tool at all. Same job, different surface.

The range behind that is real — game and combat design, UX on titles heading to release, 30-person productions, greenlit prototypes I built in code, three years of live broadcast. What connects them is the habit: make a lot of options, put them in front of real people, and let what comes back decide what ships.

Let's make something people love. Say hi.
