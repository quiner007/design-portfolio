Who am I?

I'm Quin. I build experiences that make people feel something.

Here's the short version — the long one involves a lot of rabbit holes.

I'm an Experience Designer and Project Coordinator based in Los Angeles, with a background spanning game design, UX, narrative, and production coordination. I grew up in the Santa Monica area, spent two years studying Psychology and Philosophy at Santa Barbara City College, and finished my Bachelor's in Game Development at the University of Utah — all roads leading back home to LA and to the work I care most about.

Storytelling is at the center of everything I do. It started at three years old at Disneyland and never really stopped — through games, films, music, and the stories people share just living their lives. I believe the best experiences are the ones that make you feel something real, and that belief drives every design decision I make whether I'm building a tool, leading a team, or prototyping something from scratch.

I'm drawn to the intersection of interactive storytelling and experience design — the kind of work that happens at places like Imagineering, where story, technology, and physical space collide into something unforgettable. I want to be in the room where those experiences are being defined, organized, and brought to life.

Outside of work I'm usually playing something, listening to music, exploring with my wife, or sneaking in a Disneyland trip whenever life allows. Los Angeles has always been home — and coming back here to do this work feels exactly right.

I'm also a huge music person. I've loved it ever since my first iPod Nano at five years old.

You can hear my own stuff, too — hit play on the Quintessential player in the corner of any page on this site.
