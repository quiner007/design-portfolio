Off the main path

Side Quests.

Films, music, and video series — the projects I take on because I can't not. Different mediums, same instinct: make something people feel.

Omni

A concept I developed and pitched for a short machinima film group project. The concept was selected, and a team of us created the film — from beats to boards, script to animatics, and finally in-engine rendering and animation. It was shown on stream between game presentations at the Utah Games Launch Stream.

Machinima Film Beats → boards → animatics → in-engine

Animal

My friend and colleague Ethan Hawkins's capstone film for UVU. I helped with design and direction for Nora, the main character, and Nora's room — plus conversations and mock-ups for The Observer's direction. The short film won Best Picture at a screening of 20 other student films.

Short Film · Design Support Best Picture, 20-film screening

Quintessential

My personal music project. I worked with Maya Fegyo-Judkins to produce Organic Creative, my first EP — a record about living in Salt Lake City, and a goal I'd set myself for that year. I self-produced Woody and Organic on it.

Hit play above for a snippet, or use the player in the corner of any page on this site to hear the whole thing.

The Players

A video series Will Stoughton and I created for the game/company Aglet/Onlife. The series takes a deep dive on player expression in video games, written with a comedic style. Three total episodes.
