Isometric 3D Boss Rush · Lead Designer · Team of 30+ · Steam

Beamdown

Beamdown is an isometric 3D boss rush I led as Lead Designer for my Senior Capstone, built with a team of more than thirty people and released on Steam.

The game seeks to shine in its combat, boss, and narrative design — engaging players with each Planet Guardian's boss fight and letting them choose how they want to approach it. My job spanned the design vision and the production reality: schedules, deliverables, and keeping thirty-plus people across every discipline aligned from pre-production through public launch.

The case study walks the whole path: the pillars we wrote to argue against, the loop diagrams we redrew until anyone could read them at a glance, the level maps that got laid out around each boss, and the production board that decided what actually made the cut.

2D Side-Scrolling Eat-'Em-Up · Game, Combat & UX Designer · 5-person team

Eldritch Confection

Eldritch Confection is a 2D side-scrolling eat-'em-up: your young Eldritch God must eat their way through a war-torn Candyland to power up and overcome a world of sweet, sweet danger.

Developed by a five-person team in two months — from idea to pitch, design, development, testing, and release. I led game, combat, and UX design while organizing the production process and keeping the team moving through rapid iteration cycles, all shipped alongside another full game in the same window.

The case study puts the early UI mockup next to the UX that actually shipped, so you can see what a month of weekly playtests changed — and what the survey answers talked us out of.

Narrative Sailing Game · Designer, Narrative & Producer · 2-person team

My Brother's Lightkeeper

My Brother's Lightkeeper is a narrative sailing game about community and hope, developed in two months with Kobe Riddle for Alternative Game Development at the University of Utah.

With just two of us, I owned game design, UX, narrative, production, art, and audio alongside one engineer — and we shipped a complete, emotionally resonant experience. It's the clearest example of the thing I care about most: making people feel something real.

Nine artifacts survive from it — moodboard, core loop, world map, navigation, storms and conversations, and the user flow that ties the whole thing to the theme. The case study runs them in order, so the game assembles itself as you scroll.

First-Person Narrative Platforming Puzzle · Prototype phase · Indie team

Project Futura

Project Futura is a passion project being developed by a small indie-sized team, currently in the prototype phase.

You play one of two interns who have won the internship lottery at Futura, an entertainment megacorp. The job is simple enough: every department hands you a piece of new technology, and you test it. Then you go one floor deeper, and a little further from anything that feels right.

It's a first-person narrative platforming puzzle game, and the puzzles are the tech demos — each one teaching you a tool and then quietly asking what it's really for.

Character Concept · Lore & UX Design · With Kyle Mcmullin

Kalo

Kalo is a passion project Kyle Mcmullin and I have been building in our spare time for the past six months or so.

He's a top laner who is independent and doesn't need his jungler's help to succeed in lane. I carry Kalo's lore as well as the overall UX design — a fun, focused exercise in character design inside an established design language.

No case study yet — the short version
The fantasySelf-sufficient by design. The feeling we're after is holding your own up there, not waiting on a gank that may never come.
The constraintEverything has to read as League already reads — working inside an established design language rather than around it is most of the exercise.
Where it standsSix months of spare evenings so far. Still tightening the kit before it earns a proper write-up.
